Soft late-afternoon daylight, welcoming serene mood, subtle warm sand and turquoise-blue sky palette, realistic natural geological textures. Wide composition: upper quarter mostly pale sky, dramatic mesas in the left and right edges and middle distance, unobtrusive center to place large opaque white UI cards on top. Professional high-resolution landscape photography aesthetic, not oversaturated. No text, people, buildings, logos, roads, UI, typography or watermarks. This is only a background landscape asset, not a screenshot or website mockup.
